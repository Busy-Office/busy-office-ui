## C. The finding, stated as a property

Across three items and 28 assertions: **every figure whose producing command
the entry records reproduces; all six that do not reproduce sit beside no
command.** The converse does not hold — two unrecorded figures (the 834-sentence
sweep's verdict, the 14-of-16 citation proxy) reproduce fine — so this is not
"unrecorded means wrong". It is the cheaper half of `CLAUDE.md`'s existing rule
(*"write the command next to the claim"*) earning its keep three times in one
window, and one new sub-case worth naming:

> **A criterion may not embed a citation any more than it may embed a
> forecast.** A wrong forecast is contradicted by the measurement that
> replaces it. A wrong citation returns **nothing** — and an empty diff reads
> as an answer.

No new gate is proposed. "Is this commit id the one this sentence means" is
semantic in exactly the way 94.11 measured and refused, and the base rate for
a checkable shape here (a sha in a code fence next to a claim) is close to
universal in these files.
