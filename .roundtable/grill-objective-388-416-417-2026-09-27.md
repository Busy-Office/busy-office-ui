## Finding (417.1, fixed this wake)
`overlap()` compared globs as text, so `./apps/docs/src/x` against `apps/docs/*` and
`apps//docs/x` against `apps/docs` read as disjoint. The design's whole defence against
parallel writers conflicting is this check, and the first version's 43 self-test cases
all used the same spelling, so none could see it. Now: paths are normalised
(`posixpath.normpath`, leading `/` stripped) and `open` refuses absolute paths and any
`..` (exit 2). Self-test 43 to 47 cases; red-proved by removing the normalisation, which
failed the `./` case. **Siblings not covered:** symlinks, case-insensitive filesystems
(this repo is checked out on macOS), and globs with brace expansion are not handled;
declared paths are only as good as the declaration.
