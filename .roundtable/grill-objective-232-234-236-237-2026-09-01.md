## §C — No gate proposed, and the reason is measured

The checkable shape here would be *"a count of commits in prose agrees with
`git log` over that range"*. There is nothing to key it on: the range endpoints
live in the prose, not in a structured field, and recovering them is the
semantic step 94.11 names as the wall. A regex over `\b(four|five|\d+) commits\b`
has no way to know which range is meant.

Base rate, measured before proposing anything, since that is 94.11's own test:

```
grep -c --fixed-strings "commits" ROADMAP.md ROADMAP-archive.md      # 7 · 94
grep -ohE '\b([a-z]+|[0-9,]+) commits\b' ROADMAP.md ROADMAP-archive.md | wc -l   # 79
```

**79 candidate phrases. This grill checked one of them and it is wrong; the
other 78 were NOT checked**, and that is said rather than rounded to "one in
79", because the honest denominator here is 1. What the 79 settles is the gate
question and only that: a detector keyed on the phrase shape would fire on all
79 and so is green on everything, while one that tried to recover each phrase's
intended commit range out of the surrounding prose is doing the semantic step
94.11 names as the wall — and would be red on a correct tree. **Refused on the measurement, not on taste** —
the same answer 236.2 and 229.3 reached about their own proposals, and the third
consecutive grill to reach it.

The mitigation that *does* work is already this repo's written rule and was
followed one paragraph away in the same item: **write the command next to the
claim.** The premise above the defect has one and is correct; the count below it
has none and is wrong. That is a one-item sample, and it is stated as one.
