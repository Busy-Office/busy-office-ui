## A — Slice 339's series reproduces to the word, and the next data point is this wake

**The instrument.** Step 0c is `### Step 0c` up to `### Step 1`, word-counted
with Python's `str.split()` per `ENVIRONMENT.md` §7 (a bare `wc -w` undercounts
this repo by 2.4–4.5%). Slice 339's figures **exclude the heading line**; that
convention is what makes them reproduce, and it is stated here because reading
them under the other convention gives 1,335 and looks like a 13-word defect.
The heading is exactly 13 words. Both conventions are reported below.

**The per-revision series, re-derived over the last 22 revisions of `LOOPS.md`:**

```

```

Every figure Slice 339 published is in that column: 936, the 15 flat commits,
+364, +200, 1,500, and 1,322. **Nothing is off by anything.**

**The new data point is the finding.** 339's fix landed at `f9e0f17d`. The very
next commit to touch `LOOPS.md` is collision 5's record, and it puts the section
**16 words above the 1,500 the sweep was reacting to** and **138 above the 1,378
it was originally cut from**. One wake.

**And the charter was followed.** This is the part that matters, because the
easy reading — "the loser was undisciplined" — is wrong and would send the next
wake to the wrong fix. `LOOPS-archive.md` received the 40 lines of forensics, as
274.2 requires; what stayed inline is only what the charter says stays. The
+194 splits exactly two ways:

| what | words | mandated by |
|---|---|---|
| the one-line list entry for collision 5 | **115** | Step 0c's *"a new collision adds a LINE here"* |
| correcting the counts the incident falsified | **79** (net; 36 replaced) | nothing — see below |

**So the generator has TWO outputs and the charter throttles only one.** The
115 is a floor: a wake cannot comply and add less. The 79 is the half nobody
has named — **an incident does not merely add a line, it falsifies the prose
around the line.** Collision 5 made *"Four as of 2026-09-07"* wrong, made
*"Three of the four now cost less than…"* wrong, and made *"the sample is four,
and collision 1 did cost a whole wake"* wrong. Each correction is required by
this repo's own standing rule against stale counts in prose, and each costs
words.

**The tension is real and is not resolvable by better discipline**, which is
why it is filed rather than fixed here:

- Replacing the per-incident list with a **count plus a pointer** is what Step
  0c already refuses, in its own words — *"Count them by re-reading the list
  below rather than trusting a number in prose"* — written because *"it has
  happened once"* stood stale for ten days.
- Keeping the list means ~115 words per collision, forever, plus whatever the
  incident falsifies.

At five collisions the section is 1,516 words and the cut it received lasted
**one commit**. Filed as **341.1**.

**What this does NOT claim.** It does not claim Step 0c is too long — that is a
judgement, and `LOOPS.md` Step 0c is read by every wake, so length there is not
automatically waste. It claims the *cut* did not hold and the *reason* is
structural, which is exactly 339.1's third branch arriving with a case.
