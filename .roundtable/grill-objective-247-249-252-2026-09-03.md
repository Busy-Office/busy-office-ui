## A — 247.1's base rate is wrong by 4x, and it is the sole basis for a refusal

**Claim.** *"Across all tracked markdown the same pattern hits **45** sites, and
~39 are inside `ROADMAP-archive.md` and dated `.roundtable/grill-*.md` reports"*

**Measured**, with 247.1's own regex, at the revision 247.1 itself names

```

while read f; do git show c31799a3:"$f" \
  | grep -ohE '`?[A-Za-z0-9_.-]+\.(md|mjs|py|css|json|astro|ts):[0-9]+'; done < /tmp/mdlist.txt | wc -l
```

| | claimed | measured at `c31799a3` |
|---|---|---|
| all tracked markdown | 45 sites | **188** sites, across **24** files |
| `ROADMAP-archive.md` alone | (inside the ~39) | **91** |

The figure is not merely stale — it is **internally impossible against 247.1's
own table**, which correctly reports 17 sites in `ROADMAP.md` and describes the
archive as the bulk of the population. The archive alone is double the stated
whole-tree total.

**What the instrument most likely did.** Sites in all tracked markdown
*excluding* `ROADMAP-archive.md` and `*grill*.md` read **44** at that revision —
one off the published 45, and the *complement* of what the sentence says 45 is.
The residue and the population look to have been swapped: 45 was labelled the
total when it is approximately the non-archive remainder, and the archive+grill
population (144) was reported as ~39.

**The refusal itself stands, and is strengthened.** 188 sites, ~85% of them in
frozen history and dated reports, is a stronger case against a blanket gate than
45 was. Nothing built on this needs reversing; the published number does.

## Two instrument notes, both this wake's own errors

**A green red-proof was a defect in the injection, twice, exactly as CLAUDE.md
says.** The first probe injection — 28 rules of `outline-offset: Npx;
letter-spacing: 0.0Nem;` — was 2,158 source bytes and left the gate **green** at
36 bytes of headroom, because near-identical rules gzip almost to nothing and
the budget is a *gzip* ceiling. The second, 3,109 bytes of random tokens, never
reached `check:size` at all: `build:rf-essentials` runs sixteen steps earlier
and its 40 kB **minified** ceiling broke first (`40.8 kB min > 40 kB`, against a
38.13 kB baseline). Only the third — 1,000 source bytes, incompressible, inside
the ~1.87 kB minified headroom rf-essentials allows — landed in the window the
per-file arm actually tests. Neither green was the gate's fault.

*Worth carrying:* the two size gates are **not independent**, and the order is
inherent rather than a defect — `check:size` measures rf-essentials' output, so
`build:rf-essentials` must precede it. An injection large enough to breach both
will always report the rf one.

**A whole-tree gate cannot be re-read by swapping two files.** Verifying 252's
`check:slice-refs` anchors by checking out `ROADMAP.md` + `ROADMAP-archive.md`
at the older revisions and running the gate in the live tree read
**708 / 261 / 233** and **722 / 261 / 234** — one low at *every* anchor,
which looked like a tidy off-by-one defect in Slice 252 and was written up as
one. It is wrong: `check-slice-refs.mjs` scans the **whole tree** for citations
and treats the two roadmap files only as the resolution corpus, so the two
commits landed since 252 were contaminating every count. Re-run in real
`git worktree` checkouts, the readings are **709 / 262 / 233** and
**723 / 262 / 234** — precisely what Slice 252 published, and 247.1's
**704 / 228** at `c31799a3` reproduces the same way.

The tell was there before the correction and was not read: *a uniform off-by-one
across three independently derived numbers is a defect in the instrument, not
three coincident defects in the subject.* That is CLAUDE.md's "identical value
across many inputs" rule wearing a different hat, and it is the reason Finding
E was withdrawn before it shipped.
