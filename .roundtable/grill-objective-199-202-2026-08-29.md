## D. 202's third identical clean sweep — checked, not just trusted

CLAUDE.md's own rule: an identical result across many runs is a defect until
proven otherwise. Both `scan:dead-style` and `report:css-repeats` declare
themselves `@exact` in their own file headers (postcss/browser-measured
equality, not pattern-recognition) — under this repo's own `check-selftests`
convention, `@exact` detectors are the ones exempted from needing to
demonstrate they can fail, because they aren't the class of detector this
concern is about (a heuristic that recognizes a *pattern* can be fooled; one
that computes an equality either finds a difference or doesn't). Three
identical results from an `@exact` instrument is a stable signal, not a stale
one — no injection performed here since a passing `--self-test`/`@exact`
declaration already covers it and the meta-gate (`check:selftests`) enforces
the declaration exists.
