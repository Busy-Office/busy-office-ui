## Part 3 — the controls

**Base rate.** 153 candidate lines against **4,000** lines carrying a numeric
token (**3.8%**) and **8,573** numeric tokens. Not 0%, not 100%: the filter
discriminates.

**Recall on the two motivating instances.**

- **329's** — caught, at live line 4404 (`extrapolat`).
- **301's** — **not in the live population at all.** Its body is 82 lines in
  `ROADMAP-archive.md`; `ROADMAP.md` holds a **4-line** pointer. One of the two
  occurrences the item generalises from has left the file the item asks about.

**Discrimination, by injection into a copy** — each injection asserted to land
exactly once and outside every fenced block *before* the result was read, and
the file on disk asserted unchanged afterwards:

| injected line | candidate delta | expected |
|---|---|---|
| A — `mean … extrapolated from a five-page sample` (sample + vocabulary) | **+1** | +1 |
| B — `mean … taken over all 128 built pages` (census + same vocabulary) | **+1** | +1 |
| C — `41.2 kB on the three pages that were opened` (sample, **no** vocabulary) | **+0** | +0 |

**B and C are the two limits, demonstrated rather than asserted.** B: the scan
is a *candidate filter*, never a verdict — census and sample share the
vocabulary, so the verdict stage is human, which is `94.11`'s semantic wall
exactly where `330.1` predicted it. C: **a figure that is a sample but whose
prose never says so is invisible to this instrument.** Part 2 is what bounds
that for the half that matters, by reading the whole open set instead of
filtering it.

## Part 4 — the instrument was wrong first, on schedule

- **`~\d` matched git revision syntax.** `7dacd80b~1` and `HEAD~3` read as
  approximations; 6 lines. Tightened to require whitespace or an opening
  delimiter before the `~`.
- **`\bmean\b` matches the English verb.** 7 of the 153 candidates are
  *"the control that makes the `200 []` **mean** served and empty"* and
  siblings — the single largest false-positive class, and it fires on the
  intake-control sentence every wake copies forward.
- **"sample" has three unrelated senses in this repo**, and only one is
  statistical: a copyable **code** sample (7 lines), a datum in
  `loop-metrics.jsonl` (7 lines), and a subset of a population (13).
- **A recall gap the first marker set missed, found by Part 2 rather than by
  review.** `352.2`'s own `1,384.9-2,930.4 ms **over 5 runs**` carries no
  marker from the original set. Adding `over N runs` / `N runs` / `one sitting`
  raised the candidate set 134 → 153 and added **5** genuine `SAMPLE-timing`
  lines against **12** new false positives — the census of a bounded population
  is what caught the filter, not the other way round.
