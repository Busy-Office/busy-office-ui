## B. The Objective counter is armed, in part, by the Objective loop's own follow-up — **Evidence** (7 of 26 dispatches)

**Two of the three slices that dispatched this very wake are Objective grills.**
`[164, 167, 169]`: 164 is *"Objective grill of Slices 158, 159, 160"*, 167 is
*"Objective grill of Slices 161, 162, 166"*.

The mechanism, and it is one hop removed from the one 161.4 closed. 161.4
excluded `Objective` from `CLOSES_A_SLICE` because it is *"circular — an
Objective row resets this counter"*. But a grill files its items **in its own
slice number**, and those items are built by **Continue** rows, which do count.
So `164.1`, `164.2`, `167.1`, `167.2`, `167.3` all arm the next grill. The row
that is excluded is the grill's; the rows that arm the counter are its
children's.

```
python3 - <<'PY'   # every rule-3 dispatch, and its arming set's grill-derived share
import re,sys; sys.path.insert(0,'scripts/loops'); import dispatch_status as ds
head={}
for f in ('ROADMAP.md','ROADMAP-archive.md'):
    for l in open(f,encoding='utf-8'):
        m=re.match(r'^## Slice (\d+)\b\s*(.*)$',l.strip())
        if m and m.group(1) not in head: head[m.group(1)]=m.group(2)
G={s for s,h in head.items() if re.search(r'objective grill',h,re.I)}
out=[];acc=[]
for r in ds.rows():
    if r['loop']=='Objective': out.append((acc,r['at'])); acc=[]
    elif r['loop'] in ds.CLOSES_A_SLICE:
        s=ds.slice_of(r['item'])
        if s: acc.append(s)
out.append((acc,'OPEN'))
dep=0; tot=0
for a,at in out:
    S=sorted(set(a),key=int); E=[s for s in S if s not in G]
    if len(S)>=3: tot+=1
    if (len(S)>=3)!=(len(E)>=3): dep+=1; print(at, S, '->', E)
print(tot,"dispatches crossed ·",dep,"depended on grill follow-up")
PY
# 26 dispatches crossed · 7 depended on grill follow-up      (2026-08-28, 1026 rows)
```

- Whole log: **26** dispatches reached 3; **7** of them would not have without a
  grill-derived slice. Those seven are `2026-08-19 05:57`, `09:14`, `10:47`,
  `2026-08-27 05:39`, `2026-08-27 19:43`, `2026-08-28 13:15`, and **this one**.
- **This wake is the extreme case and is a first.** In the other six the
  grill-derived share is 1 of 3; here it is **2 of 3**, and excluding them the
  counter reads **1 of 3**. Only one other arming set ever held two
  (`2026-08-28 00:49`, 2 of 5 — it still crossed on the other three).
- **What that cost, exactly.** At 1 of 3 rule 3 would not fire, rule 4 would run,
  and the oldest dispatchable open item is **168.1 — "let the dispatcher SAY
  when the chosen direction is blocked"**, filed by the *previous* grill. The
  self-arm deferred the fix for the previous grill's own headline finding.

**Counter-evidence, and it decides the action.**

- **The follow-up work is real.** 164.1 found `dispatch_status.py` blind to nine
  rows, every one a `Continue` row; 167.1-167.3 were genuine. A grill's children
  closing a slice is work closing, not ceremony.
- **Rule 3 sits above rule 4 precisely so it cannot starve**, and this file has
  recorded five separate starvations of exactly that shape. Excluding
  grill-derived slices would remove 7 of 26 dispatches and reintroduce the risk
  the ordering exists to prevent.
- **A classifier for it would be the sixth regex.** `LOOPS.md`'s own
  post-fifth-recurrence conclusion is *"the lesson is no longer widen the
  regex"* — and this grill proved that live: the first run of the script above
  used `startswith('objective grill')`, missed the older *"from the Objective
  grill, Slices 45-50"* convention entirely (ten slices: 26, 28, 41, 42, 44, 46,
  51, 56, 62, 64), and reported 4 of 26 with all four in the routine era. Adding
  a heading classifier to the dispatcher would ship exactly that bug into the
  thing that chooses the loop.

**So: recorded, not gated.** `LOOPS.md` rule 3's circularity sentence is
corrected in place — it claimed the exclusion of `Objective` rows closes the
loop, and it does not — with the command beside it. No mechanism.
