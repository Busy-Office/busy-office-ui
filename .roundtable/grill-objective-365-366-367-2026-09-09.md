## 4. The defect: an archive POINTER shadowed the archived body

**The claim.** Slice 367: *"Over the **15** `Standardize sweep, 4 of 4 lanes`
sections in `ROADMAP.md` + `ROADMAP-archive.md`, the explicit `Lane K of 4`
marker appears in **4** — Slices 345, 350, 357 and 363, the last four"*, from
which it concludes *"the write-up rule is four sweeps old"* and *"the `Lane K
of 4` marker starts at Slice 345"*.

**Measured.** Over that same stated population the marker appears in **5**:
**274**, 345, 350, 357, 363. Slice 274's section carries all four markers, each
with a figure under it — it passes Slice 367's own figure test:

```
lane 1: digit=True  ' — `scan:dead-style`: clean.** **0** dead declarations on **0** pages against **1,433** live…'
lane 2: digit=True  ' — `report:css-repeats`: clean, and the delta is what was read.** 74 source files · …'
lane 3: digit=True  ' — `report:prose`: clean, checked by SET MEMBERSHIP.** 118 documentation pages of 127 built…'
lane 4: digit=True  ' — `report_loop_prose.py`: carries the finding.** The `ratchet` block read first…'
```

**Red-proved by injection, with a positive control.** The injection was
asserted to land (4 markers removed from Slice 274 **only**; the file's marker
lines went 15 → 11) before the counter was re-run:

```
positive control (real files):          sections=15  with marker=5  -> [274, 345, 350, 357, 363]
274's markers stripped:                 sections=15  with marker=4  -> [345, 350, 357, 363]
```

The red-proof reproduces Slice 367's published answer **and its published set,
verbatim** — which is what pins the miss to Slice 274 rather than to a
different threshold.

**The mechanism, reproduced rather than hypothesised.** Seven of the fifteen
sections are **closed and archived**: `ROADMAP.md` keeps only a one-line
pointer (*"Closed — archived verbatim in `ROADMAP-archive.md`."*) under the
full heading, and the body lives in the archive. An enumeration that dedupes by
slice number keeping the **first** occurrence — the ordinary reading order,
`ROADMAP.md` then the archive — reads the empty pointer and never opens the
body. Running exactly that variant returns Slice 367's figure and set:

```
$ python3 pointer_shadow.py ROADMAP.md ROADMAP-archive.md
sections=15  with marker=4 -> ['345', '350', '357', '363']
sections resolved to a one-line POINTER (body never read): 7 -> ['274','284','290','293','301','308','314']
```

274 is the only one of those seven that carries markers, which is exactly the
count that went missing. **This is evidence for the mechanism class, not proof
of what Slice 367's script did** — that script was ad hoc and is not committed.

**Why the red-proof it did run could not have caught it.** Slice 367 red-proved
the instrument by *"stripping the digits from one lane bullet (Slice 363, lane
2), which flipped that section from 4 lanes with figures to 3"*. That injection
lands **inside a section the instrument was already reading**, so it tests the
digit clause and the marker clause. Nothing about it can surface a section that
was never opened. A population defect is invisible to any injection placed
inside the population.

**What the correction does to the conclusion.** The derived claims are the ones
that move, and they move **against** the item's framing rather than for it:

Measured over **all 47** Standardize sweeps rather than the 15 — deduping by
slice number and keeping the longest body, so no pointer shadows anything:

```
all Standardize sweeps                                     47
from Slice 208 onward                                      27
carrying the `Lane K of 4` marker at all                    9  -> 208 228 235 237 274 345 350 357 363
  of those, a FULL four-lane write-up (figure under each)   7  -> 228 237 274 345 350 357 363
  208: names the convention, 2 markers        235: lane 4 only
sweeps between 274 and 345 (the lapse)                     10  -> 284 290 293 301 308 314 320 326 332 339
```

- *"the marker starts at Slice 345"* — it does not. The convention was
  **introduced by Slice 208**, whose own text reads *"**Fixed by numbering, not
  by a gate.** The lanes are now `Lane 1 of 4` …"*, and the first full four-lane
  write-up is **Slice 228**. (208, 228, 235 and 237 head their sections
  `Standardize sweep:` rather than `Standardize sweep, 4 of 4 lanes`, so they
  sit outside Slice 367's stated population — but not outside the sentence
  *"the write-up rule is four sweeps old"*, which is a claim about history.)
- *"the write-up rule is four sweeps old"* — it is **27** sweeps old counting
  from 208, and its real shape is a convention that has fallen silently out of
  use **twice**: five sweeps between 237 and 274, and ten between 274 and 345
  (284, 290, 293, 301, 308, 314, 320, 326, 332, 339).

**This paragraph's own first draft was wrong the same way**, and it is left on
the record because it is the section's subject. A first pass grepping only
`Lane 1 of 4` reported the marker in 208, 228, 237 and 274 and missed **235**,
which carries a lane-4 marker and no lane-1 one. Grepping one member of a set to
stand for the set is the same error one level down from the pointer shadow: the
instrument answered faithfully about a narrower population than the noun.

**The decision `337.1` reached is untouched, and is in fact better supported.**
A per-lane write-up rule that has already fallen out of use once — silently,
for ten sweeps — is a stronger argument for putting the four spellings in an
executable wrapper than a rule four sweeps old would be. The wrapper ships
either way; only the sentence arguing for it was wrong.

## 5. The shape, and whether the run continues

Slice 364 recorded *"two consecutive grills, seven slices"* of defects that are
**a sentence characterising or citing a measurement, never the measurement**.
This is the third consecutive grill and the tenth slice, and the run continues —
but as Slice **336**'s member of the family rather than Slice 364's: *a number
that is a faithful reading of a **different population** than the noun beside it
names*. The instrument here faithfully counted what it opened. What it opened
was 8 bodies and 7 stubs, where the noun said *"`ROADMAP.md` +
`ROADMAP-archive.md`"*.

The generalisable part is new and is worth more than the count, so it is written
into `ENVIRONMENT.md` rather than left here: **any instrument enumerating
`## Slice` sections across both roadmap files sees a one-line pointer for every
closed slice, and deduping by slice number can silently pick the stub over the
body.** The tell is that the sections that come back empty are exactly the
closed ones.

  the `ENVIRONMENT.md` trap bullet, which is what a wake reads before writing
  such an instrument.

  They are closed, and 336 is itself a grill; re-grilling a grill is the
