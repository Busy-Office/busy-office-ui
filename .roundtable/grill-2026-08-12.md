## New gate (union of seat blockers)

1. **N1 (HIGH, runtime-confirmed)** — Row/tbody HTMX swaps leave `data-any-selected`
   and the live "n selected" count STALE (phantom bulk toolbar over unchecked rows).
   `update()` only runs on `change`. Fix: re-derive on `htmx:afterSwap` bubbling through
   the container (container-level listener for the event, or a MutationObserver on
   tbody), and correct DESIGN.md's "picked up automatically" claim meanwhile.
2. **P-1 (HIGH)** — Dropdown's `position:absolute`+z-index model cannot work in its most
   obvious destination: inside `.eof-data-table-container` it's clipped by
   `overflow:auto` (z-index irrelevant), and inside the frozen column the sticky cell's
   z-1050 stacking context caps it below the z-1100 header. The declared browser floor
   includes the `popover` attribute/top layer — rebuild the menu on popover, or at
   minimum document the constraint loudly and demo a table-safe placement.
3. **P-2 (HIGH)** — `content: "…" / ""` alt-text syntax requires Firefox 128; declared
   floor is 121. On FF 121–127 the whole declaration drops: no required asterisk (the
   R2 gate fix silently unrenders), no sort glyphs, no dropdown chevron. Fix: raise the
   floor to FF 128 (recommended — trivial honesty) or ship plain-`content` fallbacks.
4. **P-3 (HIGH)** — Specificity hole in the cell-bg relay: stripe (0,2,2) and hover
   (0,2,2) beat error rows (0,2,1); striped even error rows show the stripe, hover
   erases the error tint. Fix: `tbody tr[data-row-state="error"]` + source order after
   hover, or `:not([data-row-state])` on stripe/hover.
5. **I-1 (HIGH)** — Sticky `.eof-form-actions` bar can obscure the keyboard-focused
   field (WCAG 2.4.11, hard AA in 2.2 VPATs): no `scroll-padding-block-end` anywhere.
   Also: no rendered demo of form-section/actions exists at all — ship one with a real
   `<fieldset>`/`<legend>`.
6. **I-3 (MEDIUM, pattern-rule)** — Two NEW dead labeled controls: the alert/toast
   dismiss button (no dismiss behavior ships anywhere) and the filter bar's
   `type="reset"` outside any `<form>`. This is the exact A6/R3 failure class slice 1
   was gated on. Fix the demos AND adopt the build rule: *no demo ships a named control
   that does nothing.*
7. **R5 closure** — add `:not([data-density])` to the `col--secondary`/toolbar container
   rules, or scope DESIGN.md's claim to token sizing.
8. **I-2 (MEDIUM)** — dropdown `--danger` item is 4.43:1 on hover (light); use
   danger-strong/red-700 for the item color.

## Tensions

- **"Picked up automatically" vs derived state.** The delegation architecture earns its
  swap-proof claim for *event wiring* — N1 shows it does not extend to *derived state*
  (attributes/counts computed from DOM that HTMX replaces). The contract language must
  distinguish the two, and the framework needs one re-derive hook. This is the slice-2
  analogue of slice-1's "zero JS vs semantics" tension: the elegant mechanism is real,
  and its boundary must be written down.
- **Auditor/Platform GO vs Skeptic NO-GO** is again about union vs intersection: both
  GOs are conditional on items the Skeptic also gates on. The union above is the gate.

## [HUMAN CALL]s

- **Dropdown positioning model.** Recommended default: rebuild on `popover` (in-floor,
  dissolves clipping and stacking in one move); cost: markup change to the shipped
  recipe while adoption is zero — cheapest it will ever be.
- **Firefox floor.** Recommended default: raise to FF 128 (ESR 128 shipped July 2024;
  by Aug 2026 every ESR train is ≥128). Cost: one browserslist line + homepage edit.
  Alternative (plain-content fallback lines) keeps FF 121 but doubles every glyph rule.
- **N1 fix shape.** Recommended default: container-level `htmx:afterSwap` listener in
  initDataTables (zero-config for HTMX users) + exported `refreshDataTable(container)`
  for non-HTMX swappers. Cost: one soft HTMX-name reference in core JS (event-name
  string only, no dependency) — decoupling stance needs one honest sentence.
